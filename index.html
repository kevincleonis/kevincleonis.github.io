The thinking, not just the numbers

The strategy behind the numbers

Per-market pricing, not currency conversion

Why The same piece doesn't command the same price in every market: willingness to pay and the competitor set change country by country.

What Ran market and competitor analysis per country (TrendTrack, SimilarWeb, Meta Ad Library) to find the best price each market would bear, set localised price points across 8 markets, and repriced lines whenever the data shifted.

Result Profitable growth across the UK + 7 international markets.

Offer architecture: Pick-4 & BOGO

Why Jewellery is bought rarely. The lever isn't purchase frequency, it's order value.

What Designed the offer around that: a Pick-4 bundle customers self-curate, plus a BOGO and seasonal-code calendar mapped to demand peaks.

Result AOV up ~50% in 3 months (£46 → £70).

Find the angle before scaling spend

Why In paid social the creative is the targeting. Scaling before you've found the angle burns budget.

What Continuous angle and hook testing: 13+ UGC creators briefed and managed, my own filmed and edited creative (plus AI tools), daily kill-or-scale decisions on CTR, CPA and ROAS.

Result Winning angles identified first, then scaled profitably.

CRO before more traffic

Why Fine jewellery converts lower than everyday products. Pushing more traffic into a leaky store just burns spend.

What Continuous A/B testing (Intelligems) and storefront optimisation on a custom Liquid build: PDPs, offers, speed.

Result Conversion up 117%, supporting ~20% net margin.
